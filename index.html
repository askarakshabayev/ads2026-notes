How the semester is laid out

Lab N is published in the week its material is taught, and defended later — the syllabus calls that contest LabContest N. The gap is two weeks up to the midterm and three after it, because week 9 is the midterm and week 13 the endterm, and no defence runs in either.

Labs 11 and 12 are defended together in week 15 — that is how the syllabus lists them, as a single LabContest11-12 covering spanning trees and shortest paths.
